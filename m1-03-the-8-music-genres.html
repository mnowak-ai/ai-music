Your Map for This Article

Quick Recap
A music genre is a category or style of music that has a certain sound, beat, and feeling. Those three ingredients are how we'll describe every genre on this page.

There are hundreds of genres in the world, and new ones are born every year. But almost everything you hear on the radio, in movies, or on streaming apps connects back to a handful of major families. In this course, we'll focus on eight of them:

Pop, Hip-Hop/Rap, Rock & Roll, Country, R&B, Jazz, Classical, and World Music.

Tap each genre below to open its profile. Try to notice how the sound, beat, and feeling change from one genre to the next. Your goal: explore all eight.

Real talk: even music experts argue about genres. Plenty of songs blend two or more genres at once, like a pop song with a country sound, or hip-hop with jazz instruments. If a song feels like it fits in two boxes, you're not wrong. That blending is where a lot of new music comes from.
04

Why These 8 Matter for This Course

You just did something interesting in that detective game. You took three clues, sound, beat, and feeling, and used them to sort music into categories. You did it with your brain in a few seconds.

Here's the connection: AI systems play genre detective millions of times a day. When a streaming app scans a brand-new song, it measures things like the instruments it hears, the speed of the beat, and even the mood of the melody. Then it makes its best guess about the genre, just like you did, so it can recommend that song to the right listeners.

Keep these eight genres in your back pocket. As we move through this course, we'll see how AI creates music in these styles, how it sorts songs into them, and where it still gets confused in ways a human never would.

Check Your Understanding

QUESTION 1
Which genre is known for strong rhythms and rhyming lyrics?
Hip-hop and rap are built on strong rhythms and rhyming lyrics, with the words often spoken or chanted in rhythm instead of sung.
QUESTION 2
A song features violins and pianos, no drums, and it was written hundreds of years ago. Which genre fits best?
Classical is older, elegant music built around instruments like violins and pianos, often played by full orchestras.
QUESTION 3
African drums, Indian sitars, K-pop, salsa, and reggaeton were all grouped under one label in this article. Which one?
World Music is the umbrella label for music rooted in cultures around the globe, from African drumming to K-pop to reggaeton.
