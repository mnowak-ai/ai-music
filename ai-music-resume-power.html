Make It Even Stronger

A tailored bullet beats a generic one every time. Adjust yours based on what you actually created.

01

Name the tools.

Swap "AI tools" for the specific tools you used, like Suno. Naming real software shows technical fluency and makes the bullet more believable.

02

Name your genre.

Did you produce an R&B track? A country song? A hip-hop beat? Adding the genre makes your project concrete and memorable instead of vague.

03

Say what the song was about.

"A tribute song honoring a mentor" is far more interesting than "a song." The personal story behind your Thank You Track is a strength. Use it.

04

Match the bullet to the job.

Applying for something creative? Lead with lyrics and storytelling. Something technical? Lead with prompt engineering and AI production. Something business-y? Lead with industry analysis.
