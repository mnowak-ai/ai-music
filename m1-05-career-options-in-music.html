Key Takeaways

01

Far more people are involved in a hit song than the artist you see. Eight major careers make it happen.

02

Music careers split roughly into creators (artist, producer), business (agent, label), visuals and promotion (photographer, marketing), and performance and media (DJ, podcast host).

03

Most music careers don't require performing. Skills like organizing, filming, social media, and talking to people all have a place in the building.

04

AI is changing every floor of this building, which is exactly what the next articles explore, career by career.
