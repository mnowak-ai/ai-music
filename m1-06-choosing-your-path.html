Ten Doors Into One Industry

02

Two More Doors

🍎
Music Teacher
NEW

Music teachers lead school bands, choirs, and orchestras, and teach students to play, sing, and love music. It might be the most overlooked career in this whole course, because it offers something almost no other music job does: a salary, benefits, summers off, and a job in nearly every town in America.

Education
Bachelor's degree in music education plus a state teaching license. A clear, well-marked 4-year path.
Pay
High school teachers earned a median of $64,580 per year, with the top 10% above $104,670 (BLS, May 2024). Public school pay comes with pensions and benefits.
Look forward to
Conducting your own concerts, building a program that's yours, and being the teacher a future professional musician thanks in their award speech.
Real talk: ask working musicians who got them started, and an enormous number will name a school music teacher. This career multiplies musicians.
💚
Music Therapist
NEW

Here's the career most students never knew existed: music therapists use music as medicine. They work in hospitals, schools, and care facilities, using singing, playing, and songwriting to help patients recover speech after strokes, manage pain, process emotions, and connect when words fail. It's a real clinical health career built entirely on music.

Education
Bachelor's degree in music therapy plus board certification (the MT-BC credential, earned by passing a national exam).
Pay
The BLS tracks music therapists within recreational therapists: median $60,280 per year, top 10% above $96,600 (May 2024), with steady projected growth.
Look forward to
Moments like a nonverbal child singing their first word, or a patient's heart rate calming to your guitar. Music therapists watch music literally heal people.
Real talk: this career is perfect for students torn between music and healthcare. You don't have to choose.

Key Takeaways

01

The industry has ten major doors, including two often-overlooked ones: music teacher (the most stable path) and music therapist (music as a healthcare career).

02

Every career trades between speed and stability: low-credential careers start fast but depend on hustle; degree careers start slower but offer salaries and structure.

03

There's no single "best" music career, only the best fit for your personality, timeline, and appetite for risk.

04

Exploration becomes reality through small, free actions you can start this month. Step one beats someday.
