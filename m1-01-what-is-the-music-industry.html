02

The Definition

So what do we call this whole world of artists, helpers, and businesses? Here's the definition we'll use for this entire course. It's worth reading twice.

Key Definition
The music industry is all the people and businesses that work together to create, record, share, and sell music.

Notice the four action words: create, record, share, and sell. Those four words are a map of the entire industry. Every job in music, from a songwriter in Nashville to a marketing manager at a streaming company, fits into at least one of them.

Tap each one below to see what it really means.

STAGE 1
Create
Making the music itself
This is where a song is born. Songwriters come up with the words. Composers and artists come up with the melody, which is the tune you hum. Producers help shape how the whole thing should sound.
STAGE 2
Record
Capturing it so it can be replayed
A song in someone's head can't be shared. Recording turns it into a file or a track that can be played over and over. Audio engineers run the equipment and make sure everything sounds clean and clear.
STAGE 3
Share
Getting it in front of listeners
A finished song is worthless if nobody hears it. Sharing means putting music on streaming apps, radio, social media, and playlists. Marketers, publicists, and playlist editors all work in this stage.
STAGE 4
Sell
Turning music into money
Music is also a business. Selling includes streaming payments, concert tickets, merchandise like t-shirts, and licensing, which means letting a movie or ad use a song for a fee. Managers and lawyers make sure artists get paid fairly.

05

Why This Matters for This Course

Here's the big idea to carry forward: the music industry is a team sport. It's not just singers and rappers. It's engineers, marketers, lawyers, managers, playlist editors, and dozens of other roles most fans never see.

Why does that matter in a course about AI? Because AI isn't changing just one job in music. It's showing up in every one of the four stages:

AI tools now help people create melodies, record and clean up audio, share songs with the exact listeners who will love them, and sell music by predicting what will be popular next.

In the rest of this course, we'll walk through each stage and see exactly where AI helps, where it falls short, and where humans still make the call. But it all starts with the map you just learned: create, record, share, sell.

06

Check Your Understanding

QUESTION 1
Which of these is the best definition of the music industry?
The music industry is much bigger than artists or any single type of company. It's everyone who works together to create, record, share, and sell music.
QUESTION 2
A playlist editor decides which new songs get added to a popular streaming playlist. Which stage do they mostly work in?
Playlist editors help songs reach listeners, which is the heart of the Share stage. They don't make or record the music themselves.
QUESTION 3
Why is the music industry called a "team sport" in this article?
Even one song can involve dozens of people: writers, engineers, marketers, lawyers, and more. No single person does it all alone.
